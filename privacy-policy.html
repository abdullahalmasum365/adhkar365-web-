Privacy Policy

Adhkar 365 — Last updated: November 27, 2026

Privacy First Commitment: Adhkar 365 is designed with privacy at its core. The app is 100% ad-free, does not track your personal activities, does not profile users, and does not use advertising identifiers. The core Islamic features (daily adhkar, audio playback, prayer calculations, offline city database, and offline bundled typography) work fully offline without an internet connection. Network access is used strictly for optional features: optional cloud sync, small-town search fallback via OpenStreetMap Nominatim, and Google Play Billing.

This Privacy Policy describes how Adhkar 365 ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and maintained by Abdullah Al Masum, collects, protects, and handles information.

1. Information We Collect and Why (Data Minimization)

We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization. We only access device resources that are strictly necessary to provide Islamic remembrance, prayer schedules, and optional account synchronization.

1.1 Location Data (Approximate Location / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

  • Purpose & Privacy Level: Used exclusively to calculate local Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and the Qibla compass direction using astronomical calculations (Adhan algorithm). To strictly safeguard user privacy and conserve device battery, the app requests Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) only. The app does NOT request, access, or collect Precise Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION), does NOT request Background Location, and only performs one-shot foreground location detection when triggered by the user (no continuous background location service is run).
  • Local Processing: All mathematical prayer calculations occur locally on your device. Your coordinates are never uploaded to our servers, never stored remotely, and never sold or shared with any commercial parties.
  • Offline World City Database: The app bundles a pre-compiled offline database of over 34,000 cities worldwide (GeoNames cities15000, licensed CC-BY 4.0). You can select your city manually without granting GPS permission or requiring an internet connection.
  • Native Reverse Geocoding (Google Play Services): When you tap the auto-detect GPS location button, your device's native platform geocoder (Google Play Services on Android) is invoked locally to convert your coordinates into a city/country name for display. This lookup is handled by your operating system and subject to Google's privacy policy. Our app servers never receive or store these coordinates.
  • Online Small Town Search Fallback (OpenStreetMap Nominatim): If you search for an uncommon small town not found in the bundled offline database, the app makes an HTTPS geocoding request to nominatim.openstreetmap.org (operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation) to resolve the town name into geographic coordinates. In accordance with Nominatim's strict Usage Policy, requests transmit our verified contact User-Agent: Adhkar365App/1.0 (https://abdullahalmasum365.github.io/adhkar365-web-/; aam365support@gmail.com). Only the user-typed search string is transmitted; your real-time GPS coordinates, user account, and device identifiers are NEVER transmitted to Nominatim. The OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy applies to these geocoding queries.

1.2 Local & Push Notifications, Exact Alarms & Battery Settings

  • Purpose: To trigger time-sensitive alerts for Morning & Evening Adhkar, Tahajjud (last third of the night) & special accepted du'a windows, weekly Jummah reminders, and prayer calls at their astronomical times, plus optional Islamic announcements via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Local Scheduling & WorkManager: Prayer and adhkar notification schedules are computed locally using your device's native alarm scheduler (AlarmManager) and periodically refreshed offline every 12 hours via Android WorkManager. If optional Exact Alarm permission (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM on Android 12+) is not granted by the user, the app gracefully falls back to inexact alarms without crashing or blocking features.
  • Notification Permission (Android 13+ / POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to post reminder notifications and optional announcements you opt into.
  • Battery Settings Guidance (Standard App Info Intent): On devices with manufacturer-specific background task restrictions (such as Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, and Huawei), background alarms may be delayed. The app does not declare REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS; instead, if you choose to troubleshoot delayed reminders in Settings, it opens your device's standard Android App Info / Battery Settings screen so you can review background settings manually. The app never accesses, logs, or transmits battery telemetry, hardware stats, or device usage patterns.

1.3 Optional Account Information (Only If You Choose to Sign In)

Signing in with Google is 100% optional. If you choose to sign in to sync your data across devices, we store:

  • Your display name and email address provided by your authentication provider (Google).
  • A secure, pseudonymous user identifier (UID).
  • Your custom routine list, favorite dhikr IDs, and daily streak progress.
  • Your interface preferences (selected color theme and language settings) to provide a consistent experience across all your logged-in devices.

1.4 Optional Voluntary Support Transactions & Recognition

  • 100% Free & Ad-Free Core Service: Adhkar 365 is completely free and 100% ad-free for everyone worldwide. All supplications, audio recitations, color palettes, custom routines, and prayer tools are accessible without paywalls.
  • Voluntary Maintenance Subscriptions: Users may choose optional voluntary maintenance support to assist with ongoing server costs, bug fixes, and continuous development across three flexible tiers: 1 Month ($5), 6 Months Sustainer ($30), or 12 Months Sponsor ($60).
  • Digital Recognition (Muhsin Badge & Dedication Certificate): Supporters receive a digital "Muhsin" (মুহসিন) patron badge on their user profile and access to an optional personalized digital Du'a Dedication Certificate. Any dedication name entered (e.g. parents or family name) is processed and stored locally on your device (or in your private encrypted Firestore document if you choose cloud sync); it is never shared, indexed, or sold.
  • Secure Google Play Billing: All transactions are processed exclusively and securely through Google Play Billing. We never receive, process, or store your credit card, debit card, or banking details.
  • Developer Patronage (Not a Charitable Donation): In accordance with Google Play Payments Policy, contributions are digital software in-app purchases supporting independent app maintenance, not tax-exempt charitable donations.

1.5 Offline Pre-Bundled Typography (Zero Runtime Network Requests)

  • Pre-Bundled Font Assets: All font families utilized across the app (including Amiri Arabic, Manrope, Noto Serif diacritic transliteration, Space Mono, and Public Sans) are pre-bundled directly within the application's local asset bundle.
  • Strict Offline Policy: Runtime remote font fetching is strictly disabled in application code (allowRuntimeFetching = false). The font engine does not make network requests to Google Fonts CDN (fonts.gstatic.com) and does not transmit IP addresses during font rendering.

1.6 Android Home Screen Widgets & Device Text-to-Speech (TTS)

  • Home Screen Widgets: Adhkar 365 provides optional Android home screen widgets (Prayer Countdown, Daily Adhkar Tracker, Dua of the Day, and Quick Tasbih). Widget updates read locally stored prayer schedules, counts, and theme preferences directly from on-device storage (SharedPreferences). Widgets never run location tracking or send analytics or personal data to external servers.
  • On-Device Text-to-Speech (TTS): Optional spoken recitation/reading assistance uses your device's built-in operating system TTS engine locally without uploading user queries to our servers.

1.7 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Advertising IDs: We do NOT collect Google Advertising ID (GAID).
  • No Cookies or Tracking Pixels: We do not use web cookies, flash cookies, or tracking beacons in the mobile application. (See our Cookie Policy).
  • No Biometric or Contact Data: We do not access your contacts, phone state, microphone, camera, or photo gallery.

5. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, you have the right to permanently erase your account and all associated cloud data at any time:

Option A: Instant In-App Deletion

  1. Open the Adhkar 365 app.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Sync.
  3. Tap "Delete Account" at the bottom of the card.
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your profile, authentication credentials, and synced custom routines are instantly and permanently purged from Google Firebase servers.

Option B: Web / Email Deletion Request (Without Reinstalling the App)

If you uninstalled the App or cannot access your device, submit a data deletion request directly via our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Page or by emailing:

Email: aam365support@gmail.com

Subject: Adhkar 365 Account Deletion Request

Include the email address associated with your account. We will permanently delete all your data within 7 business days and confirm by email.
