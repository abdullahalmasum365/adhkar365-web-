Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, this page allows users of Adhkar 365 to permanently delete their account and all associated cloud data.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Fastest)

📱 From the Adhkar 365 Mobile App
  1. Open the Adhkar 365 app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Select Account & Sync.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm your choice. Your profile, authentication credentials, and all synchronized cloud data are permanently and instantly deleted from Google Firebase servers.

Option 2: Web / Email Deletion Request (Without Reinstalling)

✉️ If you uninstalled the app or lost device access

You do not need to reinstall the app to request data deletion. Send an email to our support team with the subject Adhkar 365 Account Deletion Request from or mentioning your registered email address.

What Data Is Deleted vs Retained?

Data Type Action Retention Period
Authentication Profile (Name, Email, Firebase UID) Permanently Deleted Instant in-app, or within 7 days via email
Cloud Synced Data (Bookmarks, Custom Plan, Streaks) Permanently Deleted Instant in-app, or within 7 days via email
FCM Push Tokens Permanently Deleted Instant
Offline Local App Data (Daily counters on device) Stored locally on device only Cleared automatically when app is uninstalled
